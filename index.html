What is Kaizenly?

Kaizenly helps you build good habits one day at a time — set daily goals, check off habits, keep your streak alive, and watch your progress grow. It's designed to be simple, calm, and privacy-first.

Three ways to track, because habits aren't all the same

  • Tick it off — one tap and the day is done, or count up to a target like 8 glasses of water
  • Time it — a stopwatch runs right on the habit card, for meditation, reading or exercise
  • Enter a number — log kilometres, pages, calories, or a unit you name yourself

Four home-screen widgets

So your habits stay visible without opening anything.

  • Progress plant — a plant that grows as you complete today's habits
  • Single habit — tick one habit off, or start its timer, in one tap
  • Habit heatmap — a year of consistency at a glance
  • Trend — where your progress is heading

Your data stays yours

All of your habits and history are stored locally on your device. There is no account to create and no data kept on our servers.

Cloud backup is optional. If you connect Google Drive, Kaizenly requests only the app-data scope (drive.appdata) — a private, hidden folder inside your own Google Drive that no other app, and not even we, can access. You can disconnect or delete your backups at any time.
